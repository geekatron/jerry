**Gaps (new this iteration):**

Verified: neither the rule file's inline-marker bullet, either template, nor `examples-appendix.md`'s "Common cases" section states any dedup step (FM-001-i6, S-012, RPN 336).
- **[Critical]** The install-stall re-assessment trigger — the sole safeguard against "this design is never ratified, so none of its protections ever apply to the real logs" — is gated on a literal unfilled placeholder, `~N sessions`, verified present in the design doc's own text, in contrast to the Q3 hook's trigger on the same page which does carry a concrete `~3 months` bound (PM-002, S-004).
- **[Major]** LLM-DECISION-LOG has no `Superseded by:`/reversal marker, unlike FEEDBACK-LOG's explicit convention for the identical need — a higher-ceremony log with a weaker self-correction mechanism than its lower-ceremony sibling (RT-002, S-001).

- **[Major]** Backfill-Queue row-count parity is verified only at rotation time; no ongoing lint re-checks it, so a skipped or interrupted rotation can silently and permanently drop Backfill rows with no future detection (PM-004, S-004).
- **[Major]** No periodic re-verification that the ≤3 L5 lint checks remain wired and branch-protected after install; this project's own history already shows a `--no-verify` bypass was exercised once, evidencing that enforcement erosion is observed behavior, not hypothetical (PM-006, S-004).

- Minor: cross-log alias-reuse ambiguity across FEEDBACK-LOG/LLM-DECISION-LOG in the same session (RT-005); model-swap capture-trigger-recognition reliability drift unnamed (RT-006); directory-level segment-file proliferation at scale unacknowledged (PM-007); the inline-marker "occasional sweep" backstop has no forcing function unlike every sibling residual (PM-008).
