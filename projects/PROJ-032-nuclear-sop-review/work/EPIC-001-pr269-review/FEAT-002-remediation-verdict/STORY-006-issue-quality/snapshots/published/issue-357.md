**What this is:** one of seven mechanical fixes the maintainer applied directly to your PR #269 branch (`proj-0039-nuclear-engineer`) in commit `c07033ce`. (see issues #358–#363 for the other six). Nothing for you to do unless you disagree with the fix. If you disagree, comment on this issue or on PR #269.

**What was wrong:** the skill's docs, on your branch, made false or contradictory claims about the skill's own state. `skills/nuclear-sop/SKILL.md` said the skill was "NOT registered and NOT live-routable" — while the very same PR registered it in five files (`CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `.context/rules/mandatory-skill-usage.md` (the skill trigger map), `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`, `CHANGELOG.md`).
